Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

"BarMetric" (the "App") is a training log app for the Bench Press, Squat, and Deadlift (BIG3), provided by the developer of the App (the "Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

The App has no sign-up or login feature, and does not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Training records you log in the App — including weight, reps, sets, RPE, notes, body weight, and goals for each exercise — are stored only on your device and are never sent to or stored on the Developer's or any third-party server.

2. Data Export

The App lets you export your training records to a file on your device. This file is stored only on your device, and the Developer never has access to its contents. Sharing or storing this file is your own responsibility.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google's advertising service, Google AdMob, to display banner and full-screen ads.

Google AdMob may collect information — such as the advertising identifier, device information, approximate location, and cookies or similar technologies — for the purpose of ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. This information is handled in accordance with Google's own privacy policy.

For more information on how Google handles data, please see the official pages below.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Policy

You can disable personalized advertising (reset your advertising identifier or adjust tracking permissions) from your device settings. Please check your device's (iOS/Android) settings for details.

4. Sharing Information with Third Parties

The Developer does not sell or share your personal information with third parties. However, as noted above, Google AdMob may independently collect and use data for ad delivery.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Training Disclaimer

Figures the App provides — such as estimated 1RM, personal records (PRs), and next-weight suggestions — are reference information based on your past records, not medical or professional advice. You are solely responsible for the content and intensity of your own training.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated without notice due to changes in law or the App's features. Any updated version will be posted on this page.
